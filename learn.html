LESSON 01

Budgeting Basics

A budget is a plan for how you will use your money.

Learn how to make smarter money decisions

Understand income, expenses and savings through simple examples designed for everyday student life.

↗

Income

Money you receive, such as an allowance, scholarship or part-time earnings.

▣

Fixed Expenses

Regular costs that stay mostly the same, such as monthly transport passes.

↝

Variable Expenses

Costs that change, such as eating out, shopping and entertainment.

⌂

Needs

Essential things such as food, education and necessary transport.

☆

Wants

Optional purchases that can make life enjoyable but are not essential.

◈

Savings

Money set aside for future goals or unexpected needs.

LESSON 03

Common money mistakes

Click each topic to learn how to avoid it.

Build better money habits

Small decisions can make a difference over time. Learn to recognize common spending mistakes and build more thoughtful money habits.

Impulse buying

You see a discounted pair of shoes and buy them without checking your budget. Try waiting 24 hours before making an optional purchase.

Ignoring small expenses

Buying snacks every day may seem inexpensive, but the monthly total can surprise you. Track even small purchases.

Late payments

Forgetting a bill can lead to extra charges. Keep a reminder for important payment dates.

Unused subscriptions

Paying for services you rarely use wastes money. Review your subscriptions regularly.

Spending without a plan

Using money without setting priorities can leave little for important needs. Create a simple monthly budget first.
